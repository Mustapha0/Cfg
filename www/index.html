About Syntax Tree
♧
Syntax Tree
A practical tool for exploring constituency and phrase structure. Enter a sentence, select spans of words, assign grammatical labels, and build a visual syntax tree.
Designed for students of English linguistics, syntax, and language education. Works offline; your sentences remain on your device.
